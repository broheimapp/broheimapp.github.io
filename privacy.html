Broheim Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Broheim is a joke app about the unwritten rules between friends. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

We do not collect anything

Broheim has no servers, no accounts and no login. We do not receive your data, because there is nowhere for it to be sent. Specifically, the app contains:

  • no analytics or usage tracking
  • no advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • no crash reporting
  • no third party trackers of any kind

We cannot see how you use the app, who you add to it, or whether you use it at all.

What the app stores on your device

Everything you create lives in a database on your phone, inside the app's own private storage. That includes:

  • the names you give the friends you add
  • the citations you record, meaning which Article you honored or violated a friend on, and when
  • your own name, if you enter one during onboarding
  • the Articles you mark as favourites
  • which in-app purchases you own

None of this leaves your phone unless you choose to share a card, which is described below.

The home screen widget

If you add the Code of the Day widget, the app writes today's Article, the rest of the Articles and your chosen colour to a storage area that the app and the widget share on the same phone. This is what lets the widget show a new Article each morning without the app being open. It stays on your device like everything else, and removing the widget or the app removes it.

Sharing a card

When you share a card, the app creates an image on your device and hands it to your phone's normal share sheet. From that point the image goes wherever you send it, and the rules of that app or service apply to it. The image contains what you can see on it, typically a friend's name, an Article and your own name.

We are not involved in that step and never receive a copy.

Names of other people

Broheim is about your friends, so you will be entering other people's names. Those names stay on your device under the same terms as everything else. You decide what you record and what you share, and you are responsible for how you use it. If someone asks you to remove them, deleting that friend in the app removes them and their citations from your device.

Purchases

Broheim Pro and any card packs are sold through the App Store or Google Play. Those purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google, and payment details never pass through the app. We receive no payment information. What the app stores locally is only which products you own, so it can unlock what you paid for.

Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the purchase itself:

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Broheim is not intended for children. It contains humour about drinking, dating and related adult subjects. Please respect the age rating shown on the store listing.

Deleting your data

There is no account to close and nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

  • Deleting a friend in the app removes that friend and their citations.
  • Deleting the app from your phone removes the entire database, permanently.

Neither action can be undone, and neither requires our involvement.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something this policy does not cover, we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to broheimapp@gmail.com.
