Broheim

Support

Broheim is a joke app about the unwritten rules between friends. It keeps everything on your own phone: there is no account to lose access to and no server to be down.

Getting in touch

Questions, a bug, or something that does not work: mail broheimapp@gmail.com and say which phone you are on and what you expected to happen. That is usually enough to find it.

Getting a purchase back

Broheim Pro is a one time purchase tied to your App Store or Google Play account, not to a phone. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore purchases. Nothing is charged again.

Deleting your data

Everything lives on your phone. Deleting a friend removes that friend and everything recorded about him, and deleting the app removes the lot. Neither can be undone and neither needs us.
